1. I used EasyOCR to extract text from answer sheet images.
2. The extracted text is processed line by line.
3. Question numbers are identified using regular expressions.
4. Answers are grouped according to their question numbers.
5. I created my own answer key for evaluation.
6. Each extracted answer is compared with the expected answer.
7. A score is generated for each answer.
8. Confidence levels are assigned based on the matching result.
9. Low-confidence answers are flagged for human review.
10. The output is saved in JSON format.
11. The current scoring method uses basic text matching.
12. It may not correctly evaluate answers written in different words.
13. OCR may struggle with handwriting, diagrams and crossed-out text.
14. With more time, I would use semantic similarity or an NLP model for scoring.
15. I would also improve multi-page answer handling and confidence calibration.
